# What makes coffee score higher?

In this project I focused on flavor, aroma, and acidity because these are easy categories to understand how they are important to how coffee is reviewed. My main question was : **Which coffee rating categories seem the most correlated to the total score?** 

## Flavor and Total Coffee Score

![Flavor Score vs Total Coffee Score](images\flavor_vs_total.png)

This visualization compares flavor score with total coffee score. The pattern shown is that coffees with a higher flavor score will usually have a higher total score. This checks out because what people notice about coffee is usually the flavor and flavor is usually how people remember the coffee. Even if a coffee smells good people are more than likely going to judge it based on it's taste. The table supports that and shows me that flavor is an important category in coffee reviews.

## Aroma and Total Coffee Score

![Aroma Score vs Total Coffee Score](images\aroma_vs_total.png)

This visualization is aroma scored with total coffee score. As you can see by first looks is there is a couple more outliers compared to the flavors chart. It still shows a positive correlation with total score. I think aroma is interesting because before the flavor they can smell it and could possibly change the expectations before trying it. By this chart I think it's still important but probably doesn't explain total scores.

## Acidity and Total Coffee Score

![Acidity Score vs Total Coffee Score](images\acidity_vs_total.png)

This visualization compares acidity score with total coffee score. Again like aroma there is more outliers compared to flavors. It still is a positive correlation and can be a factor for a higher total score. Acidity is something most people probably don't think of and put with a negative notion. In coffee acidity can be a positive thing and can make it more "interesting". I still believe that these charts show that flavor is the most important category.
